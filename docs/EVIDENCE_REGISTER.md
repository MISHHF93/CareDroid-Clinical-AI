## 1. Validated Clinical Scoring Evidence Base

| Calculator / Protocol | Primary Reference / Publication | Level of Evidence | Validation Threshold |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Canadian Triage & Acuity Scale (CTAS)** | Beveridge et al., *CJEM / JCMU*, 1999; Bullard et al., 2017. | Level 1 (National Guideline) | 5-level scale (Resus, Emergent, Urgent, Less Urgent, Non-Urgent). |
| **Wells Score for PE** | Wells PS, et al., *Ann Intern Med*, 2001; 135(2):98-107. | Level 1 (Prospective Multi-center) | Score > 4: PE Likely; Score <= 4: PE Unlikely. |
| **PERC Rule** | Kline JA, et al., *J Thromb Haemost*, 2004; 2:1247-1255. | Level 1 (Prospective Derivation & Validation) | 8 criteria negative: PE ruled out without D-dimer. |
| **HEART Score for MACE** | Six AJ, et al., *Neth Heart J*, 2008; 16(6):191-196. | Level 1 (Prospective International Validation) | Score 0-3: Low risk (0.9-1.7% 6-wk MACE); 4-6: Moderate; >=7: High. |
| **Glasgow Coma Scale (GCS)** | Teasdale G, Jennett B., *Lancet*, 1974; 2(7872):81-84. | Level 1 (Global Trauma Standard) | Eye (1-4), Verbal (1-5), Motor (1-6); Total 3-15. |
| **Pediatric Dosing Formularies** | *Canadian Paediatric Society*, *Lexicomp Pediatric & Neonatal Dosage Handbook*. | Level 1 (Formulary Consensus) | Weight-based mg/kg/day with adult max caps. |
