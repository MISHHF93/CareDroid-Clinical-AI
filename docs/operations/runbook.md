# CareDroid Operations Runbook

## Start Of Shift

1. Confirm backend/data mode in the app.
2. Open Reception, Whiteboard, Pulse, and Alerts.
3. Verify EMS, reassessment, queue, capacity, and referral counts.
4. Confirm the charge nurse and triage nurse can access their role home screens.

## During Shift

Use Reception for arrivals, Whiteboard for shared patient state, Reassessment for timer safety, Alerts for critical signals, Capacity for flow pressure, and Copilot for reviewed context summaries.

## End Of Shift

Open Shift Summary, reconcile open alerts/reassessments/referrals, and hand off unresolved capacity or boarding risks.
