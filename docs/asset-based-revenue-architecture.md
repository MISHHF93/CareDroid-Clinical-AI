# Asset-Based Revenue Architecture

CareDroid revenue is organized around the chain:

```text
Organization -> Subscription -> Products -> Asset Packs -> Assets
```

An organization selects or is assigned a commercial plan. The plan entitles products and asset packs. Products package market-facing value propositions. Asset packs bundle deployable platform assets. Assets are the clinical, operational, AI, integration, governance, simulation, and workflow surfaces users interact with.

## Source Of Truth

- Products: `backend/src/modules/product-catalog/data/product-catalog-seed.data.ts`
- Commercial plans: `SEED_COMMERCIAL_PLANS`
- Asset packs: `backend/src/modules/platform-assets/data/platform-asset-seed.data.ts`
- Assets: `SEED_PLATFORM_ASSETS`
- Departments: `backend/src/modules/platform-assets/department-taxonomy.ts`
- Service lines: `backend/src/modules/platform-assets/service-line-taxonomy.ts`
- Projection and validation: `backend/src/modules/product-catalog/asset-based-revenue.service.ts`

## Commercial Ownership Rules

Every platform asset must resolve:

- Department ownership through `primaryDepartment` and `secondaryDepartments`.
- Service-line ownership through the department-to-service-line taxonomy.
- Buyer ownership from asset pack and product buyer metadata.
- Pack ownership through `packIds` and `AssetPack.assetIds`.
- Product ownership through product pack membership, product-highlighted assets, or a foundation product owner for platform-level packs.
- Role ownership through `recommendedRoles`, `intendedRoles`, or pack target roles.
- Outcome ownership through product and pack expected outcomes.
- Subscription ownership through commercial plan IDs and plan-included pack IDs.

If any of those fields cannot be resolved, `AssetBasedRevenueService.buildRevenueArchitectureMatrix()` emits a validation issue and the revenue validation test fails.
