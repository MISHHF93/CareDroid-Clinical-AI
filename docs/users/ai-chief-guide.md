# AI Chief Guide

## Purpose

Use CareDroid Copilot as an AI Chief of Staff for ED operations while preserving human clinical authority.

## Responsibilities

Summarize context, suggest workflow prompts, surface calculators, explain signals, and keep outputs human-reviewed.

## Daily Workflow

Open Copilot from an operational page, attach patient or department context, ask for a concise summary, verify source data, and act only through the responsible human role.

## Screens Used

Copilot, Whiteboard, Patients, Reassessment, Capacity, Tools, Alerts.

## Permissions

Copilot use is role-gated; reception intentionally hides Copilot to reduce front-desk noise.

## AI Features

Patient summaries, queue summaries, calculator recommendations, evidence/context prompts, operational explanation.

## Alerts

AI should explain alert context but must not autonomously acknowledge, escalate, diagnose, order, or disposition.

## Reports

AI documentation, audit/governance reports, safety dashboards where enabled.

## Troubleshooting

If context looks stale, refresh the patient/department page and verify data-source status.

## Best Practices

Ask for short, source-aware output and explicitly request uncertainty or missing-data notes.

## Known Limitations

AI output quality depends on available patient and operational context.
