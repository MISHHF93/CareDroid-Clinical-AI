# Patient Flow Coordinator Guide

> **Related:** [`docs/manuals/roles/patient-flow-coordinator.md`](../manuals/roles/patient-flow-coordinator.md) covers the same role in fuller "mission-framed" detail. Flagged as overlapping documentation in the [Documentation Center](../DOCUMENTATION_CENTER.md#known-documentation-debt).

## Purpose

Reduce operational delay across queues, rooms, EMS offload, boarding, referrals, and discharge.

## Responsibilities

Monitor bottlenecks, coordinate staff/room movement, and escalate flow risks to charge nurse or ED manager.

## Daily Workflow

Start in Whiteboard or Pulse, review Queues, inspect Capacity, watch EMS and boarding pressure, and close handoff gaps in Shift Summary.

## Screens Used

Whiteboard, Queues, Capacity, EMS, Referrals, Pulse, Analytics, Shift Summary.

## Permissions

Currently maps to ED Manager or Charge Nurse depending on site policy.

## AI Features

Copilot can summarize queue pressure and capacity context for review.

## Alerts

Queue breach, offload delay, boarding delay, reassessment due, referral delay.

## Reports

Analytics, Pulse, Shift Summary, capacity cards.

## Troubleshooting

If counts differ across pages, check data-source banners and refresh backend state.

## Best Practices

Work from the oldest breached queue first unless a critical clinical alert overrides flow priority.

## Known Limitations

No dedicated coordinator persona exists in the app role model yet.
