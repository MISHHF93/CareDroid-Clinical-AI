# Quality & Safety Guide

> **Related:** [`docs/manuals/roles/quality-safety-officer.md`](../manuals/roles/quality-safety-officer.md) covers the same role in fuller "mission-framed" detail. Flagged as overlapping documentation in the [Documentation Center](../DOCUMENTATION_CENTER.md#known-documentation-debt).

## Purpose

Monitor CareDroid for safety, throughput, documentation quality, and first-three-minute response performance.

## Responsibilities

Review alerts, reassessments, queue breaches, documentation drafts, AI safety boundaries, and operational trends.

## Daily Workflow

Open Alerts, Reassessment, Pulse, Analytics, and Shift Summary. Review high-risk delays and confirm closed-loop handoff.

## Screens Used

Alerts, Reassessment, Whiteboard, Analytics, Pulse, Shift Summary, Settings, Audit.

## Permissions

Currently maps to Admin, ED Manager, or Charge Nurse depending on site policy.

## AI Features

AI documentation and Copilot safety copy identify human-review requirements and fallback behavior.

## Alerts

Critical alert, reassessment breach, waiting-room deterioration, provider wait breach, referral delay, EMS offload delay.

## Reports

Analytics, shift summary, audit log, AI documentation, service catalog.

## Troubleshooting

If an alert is not visible where expected, check patient flags, alert center, data-source status, and role permissions.

## Best Practices

Audit for closed-loop actions, not just alert creation.

## Known Limitations

Quality role is documented but not a distinct emergency app role.
