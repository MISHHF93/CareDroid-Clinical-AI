# Specialist Guide

> **Related:** [`docs/manuals/roles/specialist.md`](../manuals/roles/specialist.md) covers the same role in fuller "mission-framed" detail. Flagged as overlapping documentation in the [Documentation Center](../DOCUMENTATION_CENTER.md#known-documentation-debt).

## Purpose

Support referral, consult, and transfer decisions from the physician/referral workflow.

## Responsibilities

Review referral context, confirm service fit, communicate acceptance or delay, and preserve handoff notes.

## Daily Workflow

Open Referrals, review linked patient detail, use Copilot only for context summaries, and record consult status.

## Screens Used

Referrals, Patients, Whiteboard, Copilot, Medical Tools.

## Permissions

Currently maps to physician or ED manager permissions; Specialist is not a first-class app role yet.

## AI Features

Copilot summaries, calculator routing, evidence prompts. Human review is required.

## Alerts

Delayed referral, critical patient flag, reassessment due, transfer bottleneck.

## Reports

Referral queue, analytics, shift summary.

## Troubleshooting

If a referral is missing, search Patients by MRN and verify the referral is linked to the current patient.

## Best Practices

Keep accept/decline/delay status explicit and include callback requirements.

## Known Limitations

Specialist-specific RBAC is represented by physician/referral workflows rather than a distinct role.
