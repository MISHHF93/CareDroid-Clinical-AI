# Pharmacist Manual

**Role ID:** `pharmacist`  
**Landing page:** `/emergency/alerts`  
**Mission:** Catch medication risks before they reach the patient. Deliver clear recommendations to the clinical team.

---

## Role Purpose

The Pharmacist reviews medication alerts generated by the ED workflow: high-risk medication orders, drug-drug interactions, allergy conflicts, and weight-based dosing risks. CareDroid routes medication alerts to you as the first owner. Your recommendation feeds the prescribing physician before any medication reaches the patient. AI provides a medication summary and patient context — you own every medication recommendation.

---

## Dashboard View

**Alerts** (`/emergency/alerts`) — Filtered to pharmacy alerts: medication risk flags, allergy conflicts, drug interaction warnings, critical dose concerns.

**Patients** (medication context view) — Read access to patient medication history, allergy list, and weight for the purpose of your review.

**Tools** (`/emergency/tools`) — Drug interaction checker, weight-based dosing calculators, clinical pharmacy tools.

**AI Chief** (Copilot panel) — Patient context summary for medication review.

---

## Allowed Actions

- Review medication alerts and provide written recommendations
- Acknowledge and resolve pharmacy alerts
- Escalate to the prescribing physician when an alert requires immediate action
- Access patient medication history and allergy list (read-only)
- Access clinical pharmacy tools and drug interaction checker
- Request AI Chief `patient_summary` for patient context

## Restricted Actions

- Cannot prescribe or enter medication orders
- Cannot modify clinical triage or patient records
- Cannot administer medications
- Cannot access analytics or system settings

## Daily Workflow

### How to Start Your Shift

1. Log in — your landing page is the Alerts page filtered to pharmacy alerts.
2. Review any open medication alerts from the previous shift.
3. Confirm your communication channel with the ED is active.

### How to Review a Medication Alert

1. Open the medication alert.
2. Review the alert details:
   - Patient name, MRN, weight, age
   - Medication ordered, dose, route, frequency
   - Alert type: drug-drug interaction, allergy conflict, dose range concern, renal/hepatic adjustment needed
   - Ordering physician
3. Open the patient's medication list and allergy list in the patient context panel.
4. Run the **Drug interaction checker** if relevant: Tools → Drug Checker.
5. Review patient context: use AI Chief `patient_summary` for a quick summary of the patient's relevant history.
6. Formulate your recommendation:
   - Safe to proceed: "Order reviewed. No intervention required."
   - Modification needed: "Dose should be reduced to [X] due to [CrCl/weight/interaction]. Recommend [alternative or dose]."
   - Hold required: "Do not administer — [drug name] is contraindicated in this patient due to [reason]. Recommend [alternative]."
7. Document your recommendation in the alert panel.
8. Click **Send recommendation to prescriber**. The ordering physician receives your recommendation as an in-app notification.
9. Click **Acknowledge** to record your review.

### How to Escalate a Critical Medication Alert

If the medication risk is immediate (e.g., ordered medication is likely to cause serious harm):
1. Call the ordering physician directly. Do not wait for in-app response.
2. "This is [your name], pharmacy. I'm calling about a medication order for [patient name]. [Drug name] as ordered has a critical [interaction/contraindication] with [reason]. I recommend you HOLD this order and consider [alternative]."
3. Document the phone call in the alert: who you called, when, what you said, what they responded.
4. Click **Escalate** in the alert if the physician is not responding.
5. Notify the charge nurse if the physician cannot be reached within 5 minutes.

## Alerts You Receive

| Alert | Meaning | Your action |
|-------|---------|------------|
| Drug-drug interaction | A combination of ordered medications creates a clinical risk | Review, recommend, notify prescriber |
| Allergy conflict | A medication was ordered for a patient with a documented allergy | Escalate immediately. This is a critical alert. |
| Dose range concern | The ordered dose is outside safe range for this patient's weight/renal/hepatic status | Review and recommend dose adjustment |
| Pharmacy service delay | Pharmacy workflow is delayed beyond threshold | Notify prescribing clinician; activate manual dispensing protocol |

## Fallback Procedures

### If CareDroid is unavailable

1. Use your hospital's medication reconciliation and alert system.
2. Review the patient's Medication Administration Record (MAR) directly.
3. Contact the prescribing physician by phone for all critical medication alerts.
4. Document all recommendations in your pharmacy information system.
5. After CareDroid recovery, add your recommendations to the patient alert record.

---

## Troubleshooting

**An allergy conflict alert was generated but the physician already administered the drug.** This is a critical event. Notify the charge nurse and physician immediately. Document that you were not notified in time. Follow your hospital's adverse drug event reporting protocol.

**The prescribing physician disagrees with my recommendation.** Document your recommendation clearly in the alert. Ensure the physician has acknowledged it — even if they override. Their override is their licensed clinical decision. Escalate to the attending physician or medical director if you believe the risk is serious.

**I recommended a dose adjustment but the order wasn't changed.** Check if the physician acknowledged and rejected your recommendation. If so, their clinical decision stands (they must document their reason). If they haven't responded within 10 minutes for a high-risk alert, escalate to the charge nurse.
