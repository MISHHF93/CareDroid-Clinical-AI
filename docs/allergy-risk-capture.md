# Allergy And Risk Capture

## Goal

Capture critical risk information early so high-risk details surface before or during triage.

The workflow should make allergies, adverse reactions, anticoagulant use, pregnancy status where applicable, and major chronic conditions visible as soon as they are known.

## Collect

The Allergy And Risk Capture workflow should collect:

- Allergies
- Adverse reactions
- Anticoagulants
- Pregnancy status where applicable
- Major chronic conditions

## Triage Display

Risk information should display prominently in triage.

The triage view should:

- Show confirmed allergies and adverse reactions near the top of the patient context.
- Highlight anticoagulant use as a critical medication risk.
- Show pregnancy status where applicable and confirmed.
- Surface major chronic conditions that may affect triage decisions.
- Mark missing or unconfirmed risk information as unresolved intake or triage work.
- Preserve source and confirmation status when available.

## Capture Workflow

The workflow should support early collection from:

- Patient or caregiver report.
- Intake forms.
- Prior records.
- Medication history review.
- Clinical staff review.

Captured risk information should remain reviewable and editable. Unconfirmed values should be visible as pending risk context, but the interface should clearly distinguish them from confirmed information.

## Confirmation Rule

Critical risk information requires human or patient confirmation before being treated as confirmed patient context.

The system may assist by suggesting risk items from prior records, documents, or patient-entered forms, but it must not silently finalize allergies, adverse reactions, anticoagulant use, pregnancy status, or major chronic conditions without review.

## Safety Boundary

Allergy And Risk Capture is a visibility and collection workflow. It does not diagnose, assign acuity, prescribe, discontinue medication, determine treatment eligibility, or replace clinician assessment.

The workflow exists to make high-risk information harder to miss during triage.

## Acceptance

Allergy And Risk Capture is ready when:

- Allergies, adverse reactions, anticoagulants, pregnancy status where applicable, and major chronic conditions can be captured early.
- Critical risk information displays prominently in triage.
- Confirmed and unconfirmed risk information are clearly distinguished.
- High-risk information surfaces immediately.
