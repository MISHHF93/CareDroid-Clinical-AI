# ED Onboarding Experience

## Goal

A new hospital should understand the Emergency Workspace in 10 minutes.

The onboarding experience should explain what the workspace is, where emergency work happens, and how the hospital can prove adoption through analytics without requiring a live EHR integration on day one.

## 10-Minute Onboarding Flow

1. Emergency Workspace overview: explain that the workspace is an ED operating layer for triage, patients, protocols, workflows, AI guidance, simulations, and analytics.
2. Calculators: show how qSOFA, NEWS2, HEART, Wells PE, Wells DVT, Shock Index, NIHSS, and related tools launch from ED context.
3. Protocols: show complaint-specific protocol retrieval for chest pain, stroke symptoms, sepsis concern, trauma, and shortness of breath.
4. AI Copilot: explain that Copilot turns complaint, vitals, workspace context, and selected calculators into explainable workflow guidance for clinician review.
5. Workflows: show how command center cards launch triage review, protocol guidance, automation queues, simulation practice, and patient follow-up.
6. Analytics: show how assessments completed, calculators used, protocol retrievals, workflow launches, AI requests, and simulation completion demonstrate ROI and adoption.

## Guided Walkthrough

The walkthrough should be self-contained and demo-friendly:

- Minute 0-1: Open the Emergency Workspace and orient the hospital team to the command center, patient journey, and human-review boundary.
- Minute 1-3: Launch triage and show how calculators attach to chief complaint and vitals context.
- Minute 3-5: Open protocols and retrieve complaint-specific evidence, calculators, workflows, and simulations.
- Minute 5-7: Use ED AI Copilot to generate explainable workflow guidance, emphasizing that it does not diagnose, order treatment, determine disposition, or autonomously escalate.
- Minute 7-9: Launch a workflow from the command center or automation queue to show how work moves from guidance into action.
- Minute 9-10: Open analytics and connect usage metrics to adoption, standardization, time savings, training lift, and expansion signals.

## Hospital Buyer Takeaway

By the end of onboarding, the hospital should understand:

- What the Emergency Workspace replaces: scattered calculators, protocol searches, ad hoc workflow routing, and unmeasured AI usage.
- What it does first: triage support, calculator routing, protocol retrieval, workflow launch, ED Copilot guidance, and adoption analytics.
- What remains human-reviewed: every clinical output, protocol interpretation, escalation, diagnosis, treatment, order, and disposition decision.
- How value is measured: usage and ROI signals from the Emergency Analytics MVP.

## Acceptance

The onboarding experience is ready when:

- The Emergency Workspace overview, calculators, protocols, AI Copilot, workflows, and analytics are all covered.
- A new hospital can complete the walkthrough in 10 minutes.
- The walkthrough can run on demo/local data before live integrations are available.
- The final step links onboarding back to `/workspace/emergency/analytics` so ROI and adoption are visible immediately.
