# Discharge Workflow

Purpose: close the ED encounter with documented disposition and reduced duplicate entry.

## Workflow

1. Physician reviews patient detail and disposition readiness.
2. Referrals or transfers are completed where needed.
3. Clinical Documentation Assistant can draft a note from current context.
4. Clinician reviews, edits, and signs documentation.
5. Patient state moves to discharge/disposition.
6. Analytics and shift handoff update.

## Known Limitation

EHR writeback is not guaranteed in local/demo mode. Exported or drafted content still requires clinician review and system-of-record completion.
