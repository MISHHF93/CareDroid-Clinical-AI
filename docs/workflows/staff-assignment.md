# Staff Assignment Workflow

Purpose: reduce delay between queue visibility and accountable staff action.

## Workflow

1. Charge nurse reviews Whiteboard, Queues, Capacity, and Pulse.
2. Patient cards identify current state, priority, flags, room, and assignment.
3. Charge nurse assigns or reassigns staff and rooms when permitted.
4. Assigned physician or nurse opens patient detail from Whiteboard or Patients.
5. Reassessment and referral tasks remain visible until resolved.

## Known Limitation

Assignment UX is distributed across Whiteboard and patient detail surfaces; a dedicated staffing board remains a future refinement.
