# Escalation Workflow

Purpose: ensure front-desk, queue, and clinical concerns move to the right human quickly.

## Workflow

1. Reception, triage, EMS, queue intelligence, or Copilot identifies an escalation candidate.
2. Patient is flagged or routed to the appropriate queue.
3. Charge nurse or clinician reviews the alert context.
4. Escalation action is completed outside autonomous AI control.
5. Patient state, flags, notes, or referrals are updated.

## Current Sources

Reception escalation, patient flags, reassessment attention, queue assignment, alerts, and Copilot safety copy.
