# Patient Handoff Workflow

Purpose: preserve context when patients move between Reception, EMS, triage, physician care, referral, boarding, discharge, and shift change.

## Workflow

1. Reception creates or converts the patient record.
2. Reception handoff moves the patient to pretriage/triage.
3. Triage records acuity, vitals, flags, and reassessment timing.
4. Whiteboard becomes the shared patient state.
5. Physician, charge nurse, or specialist updates notes, referrals, assignment, or disposition.
6. Shift Summary captures open risks and tasks.

## AI Use

Copilot can summarize handoff context for human review. It does not replace formal clinical handoff.
