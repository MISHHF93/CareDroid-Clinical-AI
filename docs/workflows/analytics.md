# Analytics Workflow

Purpose: convert live operations into reviewable throughput, quality, and safety signals.

## Workflow

1. Whiteboard, queues, EMS, reassessment, referrals, and capacity generate operational state.
2. Pulse provides fast live status.
3. Analytics provides deeper throughput and KPI review.
4. Shift Summary supports handoff and retrospective review.
5. Executives and quality staff use trends for staffing, process, and safety improvement.

## Current Screens

Emergency Analytics, Department Pulse, Shift Summary, Flow & Capacity, Whiteboard.
