## 2. Detailed Milestone Specifications

### Wave 1: Acute Care Clinical Cockpit (Q3–Q4 2026) — CURRENT
- **Milestone 1.1:** Real-time Whiteboard tracking engine with WebSocket push (<500ms latency).
- **Milestone 1.2:** CTAS / ESI rule-based automated acuity scoring with mandatory RN sign-off.
- **Milestone 1.3:** Pediatric weight-based medication safety checker with formulary hard ceilings.
- **Milestone 1.4:** Inbound EMS pre-arrival notification and ambulance bay reservation.
- **Milestone 1.5:** CDL 2.1 visual authority layer, achieving 100% WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility.

### Wave 2: Regional Interoperability & Ambient AI (Q1–Q2 2027) — PLANNED
- **Milestone 2.1:** Full bi-directional HL7 FHIR R4 integration with Epic, Cerner, and MEDITECH.
- **Milestone 2.2:** Ambient clinical voice scribe for emergency physician H&P documentation.
- **Milestone 2.3:** Provincial health registry integration (Ontario Health / AHS Netcare).
- **Milestone 2.4:** Bed boarding and ALC (Alternate Level of Care) flow management.

### Wave 3: Predictive Intelligence & Digital Twin (Q3–Q4 2027) — RESEARCH
- **Milestone 3.1:** Early clinical deterioration warning (SOFA/NEWS2 score automated streaming).
- **Milestone 3.2:** Waiting room Left-Without-Being-Seen (LWBS) hazard prediction.
- **Milestone 3.3:** Digital Twin emergency department simulation for surge and mass-casualty drills.

### Wave 4: Autonomous Systems & Hardware Orchestration (2028+) — FUTURE
- **Milestone 4.1:** Autonomous Mobile Robot (AMR) specimen and emergency medication transport.
- **Milestone 4.2:** Remote northern community emergency telemedicine and drone resupply.
