# Pregnancy considerations in ED — CareDroid operational summary

Type: clinical reference  
Specialty: emergency medicine / obstetrics

## Purpose
Operational reminder that pregnancy changes differential diagnosis, imaging, and medication safety.

## Operational cautions
- Confirm pregnancy status when relevant before high-risk medications or imaging pathways.  
- Rh status, gestational age, and fetal heart tones may be pathway-critical depending on complaint.  
- Do not treat “adult female” pathways as automatically safe in pregnancy.  

## What AI must not do
- Start or stop medications autonomously.  
- Declare imaging or drug safety for a specific trimester without clinician oversight.  
- Present one institution’s OB protocol as universal.  

## Limitations
Internal educational digest only — not ACOG full guidance. Local obstetric protocols supersede. Human review required.
