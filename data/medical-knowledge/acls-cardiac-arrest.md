# ACLS Cardiac Arrest (Adult)

Type: protocol  
Specialty: emergency medicine

## Initial priorities
- Confirm unresponsiveness and absent normal breathing or only gasping.
- Activate emergency response and obtain defibrillator/AED.
- Start high-quality CPR: rate 100–120/min, depth at least 2 inches (5 cm), full recoil, minimize interruptions.

## Shockable rhythm (VF/pVT)
- Shock as soon as defibrillator is available.
- Resume CPR immediately for 2 minutes after shock.
- Epinephrine 1 mg IV/IO every 3–5 minutes after second shock attempt.
- Consider amiodarone 300 mg IV/IO for refractory VF/pVT.

## Non-shockable rhythm (PEA/asystole)
- Emphasize CPR quality and reversible causes (H's and T's).
- Epinephrine 1 mg IV/IO as soon as IV/IO access is available, then every 3–5 minutes.

## Reversible causes (H's and T's)
Hypovolemia, Hypoxia, Hydrogen ion (acidosis), Hypo/Hyperkalemia, Hypothermia; Tension pneumothorax, Tamponade, Toxins, Thrombosis (pulmonary/coronary).
