# SOFA Score Overview

Type: clinical reference  
Specialty: critical care

## Purpose
The Sequential Organ Failure Assessment (SOFA) score quantifies organ dysfunction over time. It is commonly used in sepsis assessment and ICU trajectories.

## Components (0–4 points each, total 0–24)
1. Respiration — PaO2/FiO2 ratio
2. Coagulation — platelet count
3. Liver — bilirubin
4. Cardiovascular — MAP and vasopressor requirements
5. Central nervous system — Glasgow Coma Scale
6. Renal — creatinine or urine output

## Interpretation
Higher scores indicate greater organ failure burden. A rise of ≥ 2 points from baseline suggests increased mortality risk in sepsis contexts. SOFA supports clinician judgment; it does not replace bedside assessment.
