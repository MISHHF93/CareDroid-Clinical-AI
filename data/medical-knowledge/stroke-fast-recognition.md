# Stroke recognition (FAST) — CareDroid operational summary

Type: protocol  
Specialty: emergency medicine / neurology

## Purpose
Rapid recognition of possible acute stroke symptoms for ED/EMS operational awareness.

## FAST screen
- **F**ace: facial droop or asymmetry  
- **A**rms: unilateral arm weakness  
- **S**peech: slurred or language difficulty  
- **T**ime: note last known well; activate stroke pathway without delay  

## Operational reminders
- Do not delay transport or ED activation for non-essential tasks.  
- Last known well time is critical for pathway eligibility discussions.  
- Hypoglycemia can mimic stroke — check glucose per local protocol.  

## Limitations
This is a CareDroid internal educational digest, not a full AHA/ASA guideline text.  
Local stroke pathway and neurologist protocols supersede this summary.  
AI must not independently decide tPA/thrombectomy eligibility. Human review required.
