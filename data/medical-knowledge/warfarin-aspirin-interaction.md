# Warfarin and Aspirin Interaction

Type: drug information  
Specialty: emergency medicine

## Interaction summary
Combining warfarin with aspirin significantly increases bleeding risk, including gastrointestinal hemorrhage and intracranial bleeding.

## Mechanism
Warfarin inhibits vitamin K-dependent clotting factors. Aspirin irreversibly inhibits platelet cyclooxygenase-1. Together they impair both coagulation cascade function and platelet aggregation.

## Clinical risk
- Major bleeding risk is higher than either drug alone.
- Monitor INR more frequently when therapy is combined.
- Assess indication: secondary stroke prevention may justify combination in selected patients under specialist oversight.

## ED actions
Review indication, last INR, active bleeding, hemoglobin, and recent anticoagulant changes. Consult pharmacy or hematology for complex cases. Do not autonomously stop or start anticoagulation based on AI output.
