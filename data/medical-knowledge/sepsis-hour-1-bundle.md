# Sepsis Hour-1 Bundle (Surviving Sepsis Campaign)

Type: clinical guideline  
Specialty: emergency medicine

## Recognition
Suspect sepsis when infection is present or suspected plus acute organ dysfunction:
- Hypotension (SBP < 90 mmHg or MAP < 65)
- Lactate ≥ 2 mmol/L
- Acute kidney injury, altered mental status, or hypoxemia

## Hour-1 interventions
1. Measure lactate; remeasure if initial lactate > 2 mmol/L.
2. Obtain blood cultures before antibiotics when feasible without delaying antibiotics.
3. Administer broad-spectrum antibiotics within 1 hour for sepsis or septic shock.
4. Begin 30 mL/kg crystalloid for hypotension or lactate ≥ 4 mmol/L.
5. Apply vasopressors if hypotension persists during or after fluid resuscitation to maintain MAP ≥ 65 mmHg.

## ED documentation reminders
Document time zero, antibiotic time, fluid volume, vasopressor start, and reassessment vitals.
Human clinical review is required before acting on AI-generated summaries.
