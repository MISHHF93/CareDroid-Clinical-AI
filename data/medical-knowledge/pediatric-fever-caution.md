# Pediatric fever caution — CareDroid operational summary

Type: clinical reference  
Specialty: emergency medicine / pediatrics

## Purpose
Remind staff that pediatric fever evaluation is age-dependent and not interchangeable with adult pathways.

## High-level operational cautions
- Neonates and young infants often require different risk stratification than older children.  
- Parental concern, feeding, urine output, lethargy, and vaccination status matter.  
- Avoid adult medication doses; use weight-based pediatric dosing tools under clinician authority.  

## What AI must not do
- Invent weight-based drug doses without a deterministic calculator and clinician review.  
- Apply adult sepsis or cardiac arrest doses to children.  
- Replace pediatric emergency clinician assessment.  

## Limitations
Internal educational digest only. Local pediatric protocols and AAP/PALS-aligned pathways supersede this note. Human review required.
